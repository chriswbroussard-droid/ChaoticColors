About the Artist

A Bold Departure

At seventeen, Chris Broussard left a quiet Louisiana suburb driven by a singular ambition: to leave an indelible mark on the world. This pursuit ignited a thirty-five-year legacy as a premier hair color specialist and certified instructor in the Law of Color. For decades, Chris operated at the precise intersection of pigment and light, mastering the exacting science of chemistry to manifest the visions of others.

Listening to the Universe

In 2024, at the pinnacle of the salon industry, a profound internal shift occurred. Semi-retirement was not a retreat, but an evolution—a courageous choice to stop dictating color and begin listening to its inherent frequency. By surrendering to the "Macro-Organic Abstraction" of fluid acrylics, Chris traded the highly controlled environment of the salon for a world where chemical reactions are volatile, high-stakes, and breathtakingly unpredictable.

The Art of Chaotic Color

Today, as the founder of Chaotic Color Designs, Chris channels thirty-five years of technical mastery into kinetic, living works of art. Utilizing professional-grade high-flow acrylics, Chris harnesses the raw, unbridled beauty of unpredictable chemistry. Each canvas is a celebration of a life dedicated to color—now liberated to flow, collide, and vibrate in entirely one-of-a-kind formations.
