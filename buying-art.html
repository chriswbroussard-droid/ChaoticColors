How to Buy My Art

Whether you’re choosing a piece for your home, buying a gift, or adding to a collection, you can order a signed paper print or a canvas print, or contact me about an original painting.

Paper and canvas prints

Giclée paper prints are made with pigment inks on acid-free cotton rag paper and sealed to help protect against UV damage. Each paper print is hand-signed and numbered and comes with a certificate of authenticity.

Canvas prints are available stretched or unstretched.

Choose a piece from the gallery and request a quote. Pricing depends on the print size. Send me the artwork title, your preferred dimensions, and your choice of paper, stretched canvas, or unstretched canvas. If you’re unsure about size, share the width of the wall or space and I can suggest options. I’ll confirm what sizes are available and quote your print before you order.

Original paintings

Original paintings are available by inquiry and only if they have not already sold. Email me with the artwork title and your shipping destination. If you’re comparing several works, feel free to include them in one message.

Ask about availability, dimensions, price, and delivery. We’ll confirm the details and arrange payment directly.

Inquire about an original

Shipping and returns

Orders typically ship within 3–5 business days. Allow 14–21 business days for insured delivery within the United States. Original artwork ships with tracking and insurance.

International shipping with tracking is available by request. The buyer is responsible for customs duties, import taxes, and local handling fees.

Sales of hand-signed limited editions are final. If your artwork arrives damaged, email me right away so we can address it through shipping insurance.
