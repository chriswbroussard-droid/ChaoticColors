Artist Statement

Art has been my shadow since I could walk, but it found its most potent expression through the study of Kinetic Arrest. My work is an orchestration of collisions between molecular chemistry and creative intuition. By manipulating surface tension and fluid dynamics, I capture movement suspended in time, resulting in patterns that mimic the complex vibrations found in nature.

I do not paint static images; I facilitate environments where pigments can interact, compete, and eventually find equilibrium. My deep-rooted mastery of color theory allows me to predict these interactions while leaving enough room for the organic "chaos" that defines my style. Each series represents a rigorous exploration of theme and methodology, bridging the gap between scientific precision and abstract emotion.

"Art should be an invitation to wonder."

About the Artist

A Legacy of Color, Transformed

A Bold Departure
At seventeen, Chris Broussard left a small Louisiana suburb with a singular, quiet ambition: to leave a lasting mark on the world. That journey began with a thirty-five-year career as a premier hair color specialist and a certified instructor in the Law of Color. For decades, Chris lived at the intersection of pigment and light, mastering the exacting science of chemistry to bring vision to life for others.

Listening to the Universe
In 2024, after reaching the pinnacle of the salon industry, Chris felt a profound internal shift. Choosing to semi-retire was not an end, but an act of courage—a decision to stop directing the color and start listening to it. By surrendering to the "Macro-Organic Abstraction" of fluid acrylics, Chris moved from the controlled environment of the salon to a world where chemical reactions are volatile, high-stakes, and breathtakingly unpredictable.

The Art of Chaotic Color
Today, as the founder of Chaotic Color Designs, Chris translates thirty-five years of technical precision into kinetic works of art. Using professional-grade high-flow acrylics, Chris explores the raw beauty of "unpredictable chemistry." Each piece is a celebration of a life spent studying color—now set free to move, flow, and vibrate on the canvas in ways that are truly one-of-a-kind.

Original works will be secured and sold to collectors upon request. Because of the volatile nature of the fluid process, these specific movements, color bleeds, and textures can never be replicated. When you collect a piece of ChaoticColorsDesigns, you are an elite guardian of that specific moment in time.
